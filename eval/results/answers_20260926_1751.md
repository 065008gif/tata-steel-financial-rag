### Computation of DuPont components  

| Component | Formula (average assets = (Opening + Closing)/2; average equity = (Opening + Closing)/2) | FY 2023‑24 result | FY 2025‑26 result | Tool used |
|-----------|------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------|-------------------|-----------|
| **Net profit margin** | Profit ÷ Revenue | –0.021423 = ‑2.14 % | 0.046893 = 4.69 % | `compute_metric` (Net_Margin) |
| **Asset turnover** | Revenue ÷ Average assets | 0.81636 = 81.64 % | 0.799588 = 79.96 % | `compute_metric` (Asset_Turnover) |
| **Equity multiplier** | Average assets ÷ Average equity | 2.841208 = 284.12 % | 2.975654 = 297.57 % | `compute_metric` (Equity_Multiplier) |
| **ROE (DuPont)** | Net margin × Asset turnover × Equity multiplier | –0.04969 = ‑4.97 % | 0.111573 = 11.16 % | `compute_metric` (ROE_Dupont) |

All arithmetic was performed with the **calculate** tool (e.g., –0.021423 × 100 = ‑2.1423 %).  

### Observation  

The ROE moved from **‑4.97 %** in FY 2023‑24 to **11.16 %** in FY 2025‑26. This change mirrors the shift in the **net profit margin**, which turned from **‑2.14 %** to **4.69 %**, while the asset turnover and equity multiplier remained broadly stable (asset turnover fell slightly from 81.64 % to 79.96 %; equity multiplier rose modestly from 284.12 % to 297.57 %). The numbers above are directly taken from the consolidated financial statements of the respective annual reports, with no additional attribution beyond the retrieved data.
