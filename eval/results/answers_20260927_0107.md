**Result:** The ROE moved from **‑4.97 %** in FY 2023‑24 to **+11.16 %** in FY 2025‑26, driven primarily by the reversal of profitability (net‑margin) and modestly reinforced by a higher equity multiplier, while asset turnover remained broadly stable.
