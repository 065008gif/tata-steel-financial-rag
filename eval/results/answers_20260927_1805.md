### Overall trend  

- **FY 2021‑22 → FY 2022‑23:** Revenue was essentially flat; a small net decline (‑0.25 %) resulted from a modest offset between strong growth in Tata Steel India and other trade‑related operations and a fall in other Indian operations, partially offset by higher inter‑segment eliminations.  
- **FY 2022‑23 → FY 2023‑24:** A pronounced drop (‑5.83 %) was driven mainly by the loss of Europe’s revenue (‑12,156 cr) and a sharp decline in other trade‑related operations (‑17,257 cr), partially offset by higher inter‑segment eliminations and growth in Neelachal Ispat.  
- **FY 2023‑24 → FY 2024‑25:** Revenue fell again (‑4.64 %) as other trade‑related operations and Tata Steel India both declined, while the introduction of Netherlands and UK operations and a reduction in eliminations limited the fall.  
- **FY 2024‑25 → FY 2025‑26:** Revenue rebounded (+6.22 %) as Tata Steel India, Netherlands, and other Indian and South‑East Asian operations grew, outweighing the increase in inter‑segment eliminations.

Thus, the consolidated revenue trajectory shows a flat start, three consecutive years of decline, and a recovery in FY 2025‑26, with the segment‑level movements above fully accounting for each year‑on‑year change.
