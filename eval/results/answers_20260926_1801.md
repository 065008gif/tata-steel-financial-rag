## B3 (trend)
**Q:** How has Tata Steel's consolidated EBITDA margin moved from FY2022-23 to FY2025-26? Define EBITDA as profit before exceptional items and tax + finance costs + depreciation and amortisation - other income.

**EBITDA definition used**  
EBITDA = Profit before exceptional items and tax + Finance costs + Depreciation & amortisation − Other income.  

All figures are **consolidated** and expressed in **INR crore**. The line‑item values are taken directly from the profit‑and‑loss tables of the annual reports:

| FY (ended 31 Mar) | Profit before exceptional & tax (₹ cr) | Finance costs (₹ cr) | Depreciation & amortisation (₹ cr) | Other income (₹ cr) | Revenue from operations (₹ cr) |
|-------------------|----------------------------------------|----------------------|------------------------------------|--------------------|--------------------------------|
| FY2022‑23 | 18,121.86 | 6,298.70 | 9,335.20 | 1,037.48 | 243,352.69 |
| FY2023‑24 | 6,667.04 | 7,507.57 | 9,882.16 | 1,808.85 | 229,170.78 |
| FY2024‑25 | 9,267.51 | 7,340.95 | 10,421.33 | 1,540.53 | 218,542.51 |
| FY2025‑26 | 17,001.15 | 7,167.06 | 11,954.51 | 1,401.78 | 232,139.94 |

**EBITDA and EBITDA margin**

Using the definition above, the `calculate` tool produced the following EBITDA amounts and margins:

| FY (ended 31 Mar) | EBITDA (₹ cr) | EBITDA margin % |
|-------------------|---------------|------------------|
| FY2022‑23 | 32,718.28 | 13.44 % |
| FY2023‑24 | 22,247.92 | 9.71 % |
| FY2024‑25 | 25,489.26 | 11.66 % |
| FY2025‑26 | 34,720.94 | 14.96 % |

*EBITDA calculations* – e.g., FY2022‑23: 18,121.86 + 6,298.70 + 9,335.20 − 1,037.48 = 32,718.28 cr (and similarly for other years).  
*Margin calculations* – EBITDA ÷ Revenue × 100 (e.g., FY2022‑23: 32,718.28 / 243,352.69 × 100 = 13.44 %) – all results are from the `calculate` tool.

### Year‑on‑year change in EBITDA margin

| Period | Δ EBITDA margin (percentage points) |
|--------|--------------------------------------|
| FY2022‑23 → FY2023‑24 | **‑3.74 pp** (9.708009 % − 13.444799 % = ‑3.73679) |
| FY2023‑24 → FY2024‑25 | **+1.96 pp** (11.663296 % − 9.708009 % = +1.955287) |
| FY2024‑25 → FY2025‑26 | **+3.29 pp** (14.956901 % − 11.663296 % = +3.293605) |

### Component movements that explain the margin changes  

The YoY percentage changes for each component (as shown in the `get_financials` output) are reproduced below:

| Component | FY2022‑23 → FY2023‑24 | FY2023‑24 → FY2024‑25 | FY2024‑25 → FY2025‑26 |
|-----------|----------------------|----------------------|----------------------|
| Profit before exceptional & tax | –63.21 % (‑11,454.82 cr) | +39.0 % (+2,600.47 cr) | +83.45 % (+7,733.64 cr) |
| Finance costs | +19.19 % (+1,208.87 cr) | –2.22 % (‑166.62 cr) | –2.37 % (‑173.89 cr) |
| Depreciation & amortisation | +5.86 % (+546.96 cr) | +5.46 % (+539.17 cr) | +14.71 % (+1,533.18 cr) |
| Other income | +74.35 % (+771.37 cr) | –14.83 % (‑268.32 cr) | –9.01 % (‑138.75 cr) |
| Revenue from operations | –5.83 % (‑14,181.91 cr) | –4.64 % (‑10,628.27 cr) | +6.22 % (+13,597.43 cr) |

#### How the components relate to the margin movement  

* **FY2022‑23 → FY2023‑24 (margin ↓ ‑3.74 pp)** – The large decline in profit before exceptional items and tax (‑63.21 %) and the fall in revenue (‑5.83 %) reduced EBITDA, while finance costs rose (+19.19 %). The net effect lowered the margin from 13.44 % to 9.71 %.

* **FY2023‑24 → FY2024‑25 (margin ↑ +1.96 pp)** – Profit before exceptional items and tax increased (+39.0 %), partially offset by lower revenue (‑4.64 %) and a fall in other income (‑14.83 %). The increase in operating profit outweighed the revenue decline, raising the margin to 11.66 %.

* **FY2024‑25 → FY2025‑26 (margin ↑ +3.29 pp)** – Profit before exceptional items and tax surged (+83.45 %) and revenue grew (+6.22 %). Finance costs and other income both fell modestly, further supporting the margin rise to 14.96 %.

### Summary  

- EBITDA margin fell from **13.44 %** in FY2022‑23 to **9.71 %** in FY2023‑24 (‑3.74 pp).  
- It recovered to **11.66 %** in FY2024‑25 (+1.96 pp).  
- It reached **14.96 %** in FY2025‑26 (+3.29 pp), the highest level in the four‑year period.  

All numbers are taken directly from Tata Steel’s consolidated profit‑and‑loss statements and the derived calculations are produced by the `calculate` tool; no additional assumptions have been made.
