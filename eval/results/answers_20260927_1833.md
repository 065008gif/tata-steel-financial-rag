| Item (INR crore) | FY 2025‑26 |
|------------------|-----------|
| Profit for the year | 10,885.82 cr (FY2025‑26 Annual Report, consolidated profit‑and‑loss, p. F144) |
| Total equity – 31 Mar 2025 (opening) | 91,352.78 cr (FY2025‑26 Annual Report, consolidated balance sheet, p. F143) |
| Total equity – 31 Mar 2026 (closing) | 103,780.45 cr (FY2025‑26 Annual Report, consolidated balance sheet, p. F143) |
| **Average equity** (opening + closing ÷ 2) | 97,566.62 cr (computed from the two equity figures) |
| **ROE** = Profit ÷ Average equity | **11.1573 %** (computed from the reported figures) |

**How the ROE was computed**

1. Average equity = (91,352.78 + 103,780.45) ÷ 2 = 97,566.62 cr (calculated).  
2. ROE = 10,885.82 ÷ 97,566.62 = 0.111573 (calculated).  
3. Expressed as a percentage: 0.111573 × 100 = **11.1573 %** (calculated).

All numbers are taken directly from the FY 2025‑26 consolidated financial statements; the ROE result is derived solely from those reported figures using the calculation shown above.
